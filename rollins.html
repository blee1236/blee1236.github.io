    Company

    Rollins is the world's largest pest and termite control company by revenue, best known for its Orkin brand. Other brands include HomeTeam Pest Defense, Northwest Exterminating, Clark Pest Control, Waltham, Critter Control, Western Pest Services and Fox Pest Control. Revenue is largely recurring residential and commercial service contracts, with growth from pricing, new customers and steady tuck-in acquisitions. Headquartered in Atlanta.
