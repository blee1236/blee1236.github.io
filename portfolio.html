How this is calculated

  • Every purchase is measured from its own buy date to today, using total returns (dividends reinvested) for the stocks, the S&P 500 total-return index and QQQ. Weights are shares of current market value.
  • Beta comes from weekly returns against the S&P 500 over each position's holding period, with at least two years of history. The beta-predicted return compounds the T-bill rate plus beta times the market's excess return day by day; alpha is the actual return minus that.
  • Alpha per year is only shown for positions held at least 1.5 years; annualizing a few months of returns mostly measures noise. † marks a beta with almost no statistical fit.
  • This is a single-factor model: it adjusts for market risk, not for sector, size or momentum, so part of the alpha in semiconductors and other hot sectors may be a sector effect.
  • Covers positions currently held in one personal brokerage account; closed positions, options and retirement accounts are excluded. Prices from Yahoo Finance and may be delayed.
